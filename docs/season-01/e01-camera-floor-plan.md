# Æclyptic E01 · Nia/Vesper Camera Floor Plan v0.1
**Stage:** PREVIS DRAFT; numerical coordinates are staging proposals, not surveyed physical measurements. All shot IDs refer to the existing 40-second cross-scene visual-language test.

## Set geometry (meters)
Rectangular 6 m E–W × 5 m S–N recording studio. Origin (0,0) = southwest interior corner. +x East, +y North; +z vertically up from floor. Desk centered on (3.0,2.5), editing monitor on north side. Nia's standing/sitting eyeline anchor: (2.50,2.10,1.55). Vesper's subjective appearance anchor: (3.70,3.20,1.58), diagonally across Nia's desk. Vesper's spatial mark must stay fixed in both subjective and objective versions; **Vesper's face and body are visible to viewers only in subjective shots**.

## 180-degree screen direction
The line of action is the horizontal line from Nia (2.50,2.10) to Vesper mark (3.70,3.20). Keep all listed cameras to the **southern/eastern camera-side half-plane** relative to this axis. The audience should always read Nia as looking screen-right to Vesper and Vesper as looking screen-left toward Nia, unless motivated camera movement explicitly crosses the line and supplies an orientation shot.

## Performance coverage
- Nia looks into Vesper's eyes and receives expressive reactions. Use alternating two-shot and complementary close-ups, never reduce Vesper to a waveform or voiceover.
- Hold Vesper's reaction on “I don't know how to answer without lying,” and maintain a close-up that lets viewers recognize hesitation.
- The S10 -> S11 match cut must preserve Nia's exact gaze destination, hand extension, wardrobe, desk props, screen direction and camera room geography. Only the subjective Vesper and Nexus effects disappear.
- Do not let glowing Nexus imagery cast measurable light or shadows in the neutral objective coverage. Keep lighting consistent enough to match without eliminating the subjective mood.
- External characters cannot hear Vesper. The **viewer must** hear and see her in subjective shots.
