# CinemaSwarm screenplay production rules

These rules recover the cross-chat writing decisions for The Æclyptic. Use them with the [series bible](series-bible.md), [semantic dramaturgy](../semantic-dramaturgy.md), [narrative morphology](../narrative-morphology.md), [symbolic narrative](../symbolic-narrative.md), and [audiovisual continuity](../av-continuity.md). Apply them before locking a script, during coverage and performance breakdown, and again on the edited cut.

## Narrative and plot tension

- Open inside a conflict already in motion. Establish the immediate want, obstacle, and stakes through action and dialogue. Compress setup so the episode can deliver reversals, decisions, outcomes, and a consequence that carries forward.
- For Nia's first activation in Totality, the fictional sequence is trauma → her dormant “junk DNA” activates → Nexus comes online → Nia uses it in the emergency. Give the sequence one rapid beat. Nia is the sole Nexus bearer in the season. Other awakened characters keep their distinct abilities, limits, and costs.
- For each consequential beat, track dramatic function, character role, outcome, and prior cause. Escalate by narrowing options, changing leverage, or making a choice cost something. End a sequence on a changed situation that drives the next action.
- Inherit Phantom Kernel's three-thread braid: personal life, investigation, and consequence. A major scene should advance at least two. Give ensemble members independent objectives and a contribution capable of changing the outcome.
- Increase information density through dialogue that does several jobs. A reply can reveal a fact, expose an allegiance, and force a decision. Use interruption, evasion, bargaining, and silence where they express character. Cut exchanges that repeat information or delay the turn.
- Track what each character and the audience know at each beat. Use dramatic irony when the audience has the advantage and audience mystery when a character has it. A recognition can reverse a plan or relationship in the same beat.

## Literary and symbolic devices

- Weave devices into the causal flow. Prologue, epilogue, flashback, and frame narrative should orient or recontextualize present action. A recurrence should change what the audience understands or what a character can do.
- Use foreshadowing and setup/payoff to plant evidence. Use motifs, refrains, leitmotifs, and callbacks to return with changed meaning. Use chiasmus and inversion to return to an earlier action in a changed order or with a changed choice. Anamnesis can bring recovered recognition into the present. A palimpsest can let the earlier state remain legible beneath a later one.
- Design for literal action, dramatic function, and symbolic meaning together. Sound, blocking, prop state, framing, and timing can carry the device. Record seed, recurrence, reveal policy, payoff obligation, and any evidence the cut needs.

## Continuity and performance

- Keep a beat ledger for world time and place, identities and embodiment, knowledge and beliefs, relationships, abilities and costs, prop ownership and provenance, and unresolved payoffs. Track THE RING from its 2017 Tortola source into Nia's studio. Distinguish the atmospheric halo from the corona at totality.
- Keep Vesper visible and expressive in Nia's subjective coverage. In ordinary coverage, other characters react to signals they can perceive. Carry the perspective rule through eyelines, blocking, camera direction, and the sound mix.
- For each line, bind the speaker, playable intention, concealed feeling or information, physical action, and listener's cue. Reconcile actor marks, prop hands, movement, and reaction timing across coverage. Lock the dialogue take before generating mouth animation. Reuse that audio across angles, and review speaker attribution, lip closure, breaths, reactions, and captions against the cut.
- Check the major beats for cause and consequence, audience knowledge, agency, and continuity. Review rendered performances and symbolic cues separately from the declared metadata.

## Draft boundaries

The checked-in Totality screenplay and shot plan predate the latest pacing and activation direction. Apply this contract when revising them. The discharge-slip and recovery-ward examples from the writing discussion are exploratory scene pitches pending story approval.
