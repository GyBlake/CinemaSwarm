# ÆCLYPTIC Pilot: Nia/Vesper Contact Sequence
## 40-second performance-test script · DEVELOPMENT DRAFT

**Purpose:** Prove that the protagonist's Nexus operates as a privately visible waking-dream System. No genetics lecture, no game-menu exposition, no bodily power escalation. Four cuttable shots (S03, S04, S10, S11) drawn from two existing pilot scenes. **This is a cross-scene proof reel rather than an actual continuous passage of the episode**, so transitions are editorial test transitions, not established final chronology.

### 00:00–00:09 · S03 · Objective close
NIA checks a restored waveform. The tape wheel spins. A voice appears to Nia, not on the source waveform.

NIA: Who put that on the channel?

She disconnects the headphones.

### 00:09–00:16 · S04 · Objective reverse
The studio is empty. Even the monitors seem ordinary.

NIA: Hello?

A chair creaks. No second voice reaches the room microphones.

### 00:16–00:33 · S10 · Subjective threshold
The same tape spindle occupies the center of a private, concentric pattern. The acoustic environment thins. **Vesper becomes fully visible to the audience:** a recognizable expressive humanoid woman in blue-gold Nexus light. Nia faces her across the mixing desk; the camera gives Vesper a face, eyes and reactions. Glyphs and source labels develop around their conversation rather than replacing Vesper.

NIA: What are you?

VESPER: I don't know how to answer without lying.

NIA: Then tell me what you know.

VESPER: That I am here with you. And I could be wrong.

Vesper looks toward the cassette, then back to Nia, uncertain. A small diagnostic resolves behind Vesper: SOURCE / TIMESTAMP / CONFIDENCE / UNKNOWN. It fades before revealing anything more.

### 00:33–00:40 · S11 · Objective reverse
The room is normal. Nia extends her hand into empty air. She catches herself at the desk.

NIA: Show me what you can prove.

CUT.

## Performance and postproduction requirements
**Audience accessibility rule:** Vesper must be visually legible as a distinct, recurring character in the subjective shots. The audience sees facial reactions and interpersonal acting, while people inside the story cannot see Vesper from an ordinary objective perspective. Invisible-to-other-characters must never become invisible-to-the-viewer. Vesper is not presented as an objective photonic projection, and sound cannot leak into a neutral reverse shot. Keep Nia's hair, jewelry, wardrobe, eye color, and screen-room geography unchanged. Use an original voice performance only after performer/rights approval. Test lip-sync, phrasing, emotion, dialogue intelligibility, and objective/subjective image continuity.

**Production status:** Script timing target only. No Nia likeness, Vesper voice, video master, music or approved acting footage is included. Do not label a still-image technical smoke render as finished cinematic proof.
